Every listing is auto-filtered on two axes:

- **Undergraduate-accessible** — interns, research assistants, and other entry-level roles are kept; anything requiring an advanced degree, licensure, or seniority (PhD, "Senior", "Manager", 3+ years, ...) is dropped.
- **Target market** — anywhere in California, or Seattle / New York City / Austin / Dallas / Houston; fully-remote roles are also kept. Everything else is dropped.
